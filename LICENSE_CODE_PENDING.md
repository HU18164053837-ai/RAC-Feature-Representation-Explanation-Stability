## Options for rights-holder review

| Option | Main conditions | Suitability |
|---|---|---|
| MIT | Retain copyright and permission notices; warranty/liability disclaimer; broad reuse including commercial use | Short, familiar permission notice if minimal conditions are preferred |
| BSD-3-Clause | Retain notices/conditions/disclaimer in source and binary redistribution; names cannot endorse derived products without written permission | Recommended for consideration here because the explicit non-endorsement condition fits an academic research repository |

Both are permissive licenses, not warranties of scientific validity. Neither establishes ownership, automatically covers third-party material, nor supplies an express patent grant. This is a recommendation, not adoption or a legal ownership determination. Official texts: https://opensource.org/license/mit and https://opensource.org/license/bsd-3-clause .

Publication approval and AI-use disclosure are not copyright-license approvals.
